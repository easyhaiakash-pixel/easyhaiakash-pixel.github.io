Privacy Policy – Circle Escape Rings

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app Circle Escape Rings ("the app", "we", "us"), published on Google Play by its developer, handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or photos on our own servers. Everything you make in the app stays on your device:

  • The videos you create and the settings and templates you save are stored only on your phone.
  • Music files you choose to import are read from your phone only to play them in your video and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Saving a video asks for permission to add it to your photo gallery. We only write the video there; we do not read your other photos.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF v2). If you do not consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings ("Privacy options"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Purchases (Pro)

If you buy Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that your purchase is active, so Pro features can be unlocked.

4. Crash and usage data

If we add crash reporting or anonymous usage statistics (for example, which templates are used), this policy will be updated before it is switched on, and it will follow your consent choice.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play) for the purposes described. When you share a video, it goes to the app you choose (for example YouTube or Instagram) under that service's own privacy policy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data (Settings → Apps → Circle Escape Rings → Storage → Clear data) deletes it. Videos you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. For data held by Google for advertising, use the controls described in section 2 or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com.

8. Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or limit the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are best handled through the controls above; you can also contact us at any time.

9. Security

We use the security features of Android and Google's services. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we do not keep your data on our own servers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. Important changes will be shown in the app.
