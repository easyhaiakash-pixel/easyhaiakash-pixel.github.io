Dig Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Dig Drop ("the game") handles information. The game is published by Aakash Kumar ("we", "us"). Contact: aakashyadav.dev@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your game progress (levels, coins, levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or the iOS IDFA),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about ad interactions and app usage,
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF v2) before any ads or analytics are requested. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you will see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.

4. Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases (for example removing ads), they are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and managed with the purchase service RevenueCat, which sees an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status so it can unlock what you bought. We do not receive or store your payment details. Prices are shown by the store in your local currency.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game may send a limited number of local reminders. They are created on your device; no personal data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game or in your phone settings.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Data security and retention

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We do not store any personal data on our own servers.

8. Your rights and deletion

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. To request deletion of data or ask any question about privacy, email aakashyadav.dev@gmail.com. Under the GDPR you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
