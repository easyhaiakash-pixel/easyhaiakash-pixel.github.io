Day Card – Delete your data

Day Card is published by Aakash Kumar.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Day Card.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my online data and confirm.

This deletes at once: your online account (anonymous or Google-linked), friend-circle entries, progress, leaderboard entries and your backup. Data that is only on your phone (challenges, cards, photos) is removed when you uninstall the app.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app, email aakashyadav.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Day Card – delete my data" and the Google email you linked (if any). We delete your data within 30 days. Nothing is kept after deletion.
