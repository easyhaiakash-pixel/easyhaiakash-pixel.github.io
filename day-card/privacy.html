Day Card – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile app Day Card ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Aakash Kumar ("we", "us"). Contact: aakashyadav.dev@gmail.com.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Day Card works without an account. Your challenges, cards, streaks, notes, photos you add to cards and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Photos are never uploaded by us.

2. Optional online features (Google Firebase)

Some features use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, servers in the EU). They start only when you use them:

  • Anonymous ID: a random user ID is created so online features can work. It does not contain your name or email.
  • Friend circles: your nickname, challenge name, day number, streak and whether you made today's card are shared with the friends in your circle (up to 5 people who joined with your code).
  • Live counter, percent rank and leaderboards: your challenge type, day number and streak are counted with other users. Leaderboards are opt-in; you can turn them off in Settings. If you allow it, your nickname and Google photo can be shown there.
  • Google backup (optional): if you link your Google account, we receive your Google name, email and profile photo, and we store a backup of your challenge list (text only, no photos) so you can restore it on a new phone. An optional date of birth is stored only on your device and in your own backup.

Firebase data is processed by Google: Firebase privacy and security.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about ad interactions and app usage,
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. If you buy "Unlock everything", no ads are shown.

4. Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you will see non-personalised ads.

5. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app sends a daily reminder created on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security and retention

All data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Online data is kept until you delete it or for 12 months after your last use, whichever comes first.

9. Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my online data removes your friend-circle entries, progress, leaderboard entries, backup and online account at once. You can also ask by email: see Delete your data.

10. Your rights

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing aakashyadav.dev@gmail.com, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
