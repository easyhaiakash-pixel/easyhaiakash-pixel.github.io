Pin Rescue – Delete Your Data

Pin Rescue is a game by Aakash Kumar. It has no accounts, and we do not keep any of your personal data on our own servers.

How to delete your data

  1. Game progress: your levels, coins, heroes and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstall Pin Rescue (or clear its app data in your phone's settings) and they are deleted immediately.
  2. Advertising data: ads are provided by Google AdMob. Reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID). You can also change your ad consent in the game at Settings → Privacy options.
  3. Anything else: email aakashyadav.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Pin Rescue – delete my data". We will reply and act on the request within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all game progress on your device, and any data linked to your request that we hold.
  • Kept by us: nothing. We keep no personal data on our servers.
  • Google AdMob data is handled and retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (how long Google keeps data).
